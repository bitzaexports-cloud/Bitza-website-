Green Cardamom Specifications

Premium Alleppey Green Cardamom pods, graded by diameter, moisture, and color profiles.

Spices & Food Products

Green Cardamom

Our Green Cardamom is sourced from the misty, high-altitude plantations of the Western Ghats in Kerala (historically traded as Alleppey Green Cardamom). Hand-picked and sun-dried to maintain a rich green color, plump capsules, and volatile oil content.

Available Export Grades

AGEB (Alleppey Green Extra Bold) Capsule diameter: 8mm and above. Deep green, fully matured pods with zero blemishes.
AGB (Alleppey Green Bold) Capsule diameter: 7mm - 8mm. Highly aromatic, uniform sizing, premium grading.
AGS (Alleppey Green Superior) Capsule diameter: 6mm - 7mm. Standard graded cardamom, excellent flavor yield.
Mixed Grades Sizes 6mm, 7mm, 8mm mixed based on destination market request.

Packaging Configurations

Carton Packs 5 kg and 10 kg export-grade cartons with inside airtight poly-liners (to preserve aroma).
Export Cartons 25 kg heavy-duty corrugated export cartons for volume sea shipments.
Vacuum Packs Vacuum-sealed retail packs to survive long transits without degradation.
Private Label Custom box branding and packaging dimensions matching your retail client criteria.

Technical Specifications

Origin Western Ghats, Kerala, India
Moisture Content 11% - 12% max (monitored via digital sensors)
Volatile Oil Content Min 1.5% to 2.5%
Port of Loading Cochin Port (COK), Kerala, India
