Black Pepper Specifications

Premium grade Malabar Black Pepper, processed and graded for international export standards.

Spices & Food Products

Kerala Black Pepper

Known historically as "Black Gold", Malabar Black Pepper from Kerala is globally celebrated for its high density, complex heat profiles, and rich concentration of piperine essential oils.

Available Export Grades

MGE (Malabar Garbled Extra) Density: 1.56 GL, highly cleaned peppercorns with uniform size.
TGEB (Tellicherry Garbled Extra Bold) Size: 360+ GL, hand-sorted bold berries from selected Tellicherry estates.
Garbled / Ungarbled Standard commercial grade sorted by sieve size and density tables.

Packaging Configurations

Jute Bags 50 kg standard jute bags with inner poly-liners (PP) to withstand moisture.
Cotton & PP Bags 25 kg and 50 kg bags, double-stitched for mechanical durability.
Retail Options Vacuum-sealed 1 kg and 5 kg food-grade retail pouches.
Private Label Supported upon custom agreement (design validation, printed pouches, bulk shipping).

Technical Specifications

Origin Kerala, India (Direct farm-sourcing)
Moisture Content 11% - 13.5% max (humidity monitored)
Admixture 0.5% max
Port of Loading Cochin Port (COK), Kerala, India
